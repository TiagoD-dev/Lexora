## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the ethos of a venerated Portuguese legal institution fused with contemporary digital precision. It marries the solemn dignity, gravity, and trust of traditional jurisprudence (*Direito Português*) with the responsiveness, clarity, and friction-free ergonomics of elite professional software.

### Tone & Personality
- **Sober & Authoritative:** Instills immediate confidence, procedural rigor, and scholarly discretion.
- **Refined & Noble:** Evokes heavyweight artisanal paper, warm natural light over mahogany libraries, and polished brass fixtures without falling into skeuomorphic nostalgia.
- **Cognitively Restful:** Prioritizes dense, structured legal text legibility, reducing ocular strain during prolonged analysis of dossiers, jurisprudence, and procedural deadlines (*prazos processuais*).

### Aesthetic Classification
**Editorial Neo-Corporate**: Characterized by warm parchment-tinted surfaces, deep oxblood structural accents, restrained gilded focus states, disciplined micro-strokes, and quiet, diffused elevation layers.

## Colors

The palette relies on organic, pigment-rich foundations rather than sterile cold grays. Color assignment reflects semantic and structural hierarchy:

### Light Mode Foundations
- **Primary (`#7A1620` - Vinho Institucional):** Primary actions, active navigation states, authoritative markers, key interactive triggers.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#B4872B` - Dourado Nobre):** Status indicators, premium badge icons, metadata highlights, active timeline milestones.
- **Canvas / Root (`#F7F1E4` - Pergaminho Suave):** Replaces clinical cool-white background canvas, delivering an editorial tactile impression.
- **Surfaces (`#FFFFFF` - Branco Puro):** Cards, modals, drawers, and form input containers for pristine reading contrast.
- **Neutral Primary (`#241512` - Ébano Profundo):** Headings, critical metrics, high-emphasis text, primary icons.
- **Neutral Secondary (`#7A6A5B` - Castanho Neutro):** Subheads, metadata, placeholder text, structural labels.
- **Dividers & Strokes:** Low-contrast architectural containment via `#E6DCCD` (subtle) and `#DFD3C3` (structural boundaries).

### Dark Mode Semantic Shifts
When inverted, the design system avoids pure `#000000` in favor of warm, low-luminance deep tones:
- **Canvas:** `#1A1210`
- **Surfaces:** `#261B18`
- **Primary:** `#E2707A`
- **Secondary Accent:** `#CBA146`
- **Typography Primary:** `#F5EFEB`
- **Typography Secondary:** `#A8988B`

### Procedural & Legal Status Colors
- **Success / Deferimento:** Text `#286846`, Background `#E1F1E7`, Border `#A3D4B6`.
- **Warning / Tramitação Pendente:** Text `#876314`, Background `#FFF1CE`, Border `#E8CA7C`.
- **Urgent / Prazo Peremptório / Error:** Text `#9B403D`, Background `#FCE8E7`, Border `#EAA8A6`.

## Typography

The design system uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** uniformly across all typographical roles. Its sharp geometry, generous x-height, and precise humanist apertures provide crystal-clear articulation for dense legal citations, judicial case IDs (e.g., *Proc. n.º 1234/23.8T8LSB*), and tabular jurisprudence listings.

### Usage Directives
- **Case Sensitivity & Acronyms:** For statutory codes, procedural references (*C.P.C.*, *C.P.*), and uppercase metadata tags, utilize `label-sm` with explicit uppercase transformation and tracking.
- **Numerals:** Apply tabular lining (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) across all financial calculations, procedural deadlines, and legal fee tracking structures.
- **Reading Rhythm:** In multi-paragraph legal brief summaries or judicial analysis panels, clamp the container width to `68ch` to maintain optimal ergonomic scanning velocity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a disciplined 8px baseline grid designed to balance dense statutory data with architectural composure.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Split Master-Detail Workspace:** A persistent primary navigation rail (desktop: `72px` collapsed or `260px` expanded) alongside dual-pane panels for legal analysis (case list on the left, procedural dossier review on the right).
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `24px` gutter, `32px` outer section margins. Content containers max out at `1440px` to avoid horizontal drift on ultrawide lawyer workstations.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with collapsing secondary metadata bars into slide-over sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stack with bottom navigation bar; margins condense to `16px`.

### Spacing Application
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between status badge icons and labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Distance between metadata labels and corresponding values.
- `space-md` (16px): Internal cell padding for legal registers, default button padding, and standard stack gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Card interior padding and segment grouping margins.
- `space-xl` (36px): Outer boundary separations between primary legal dossiers and utility cards.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an austere, physical "dossier on desk" sensation, elevation avoids dramatized drop shadows or floating 3D tricks. Instead, depth is articulated through **tonal stratification and warm, diffused ambient occlusion**.

### Layering Hierarchy
1. **Foundation Level (Canvas):** The warm parchment ground (`#F7F1E4`) acts as the desk surface.
2. **Resting Surfaces (Cards, Tables, Main Containers):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px continuous stroke of `#E6DCCD` and a soft ambient shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.04), 0px 4px 12px rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.02)`.
3. **Elevated Surfaces (Popovers, Select Menus, Dropdowns):** Pure surface white with a reinforced outline (`#DFD3C3`) and directional depth: `0px 4px 16px rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.08), 0px 2px 4px rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.04)`.
4. **Modal Dialogues & Drawer Overlays:** Suspended over a warm tinted backdrop filter (`background-color: rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`). Shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(36, 21, 18, 0.16)`.

## Shapes

The system relies on an intentional curvature of **14px to 16px (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`)** for primary cards and elevated containers. This structural curve softens the dense, rigid nature of legal documents while maintaining architectural gravity.

### Shape Scale
- **Small Controls (Buttons, Text Inputs, Segmented Controls):** `8px` (`0.5rem` / `rounded-md`) for tailored, crisp ergonomics.
- **Badges & Micro Chips:** `6px` (`0.375rem`) or fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) exclusively for state badges (e.g., *Em Julgamento*, *Concluso*).
- **Cards, Dossier Tiles, Modals:** `14px` (`0.875rem`) to `16px` (`1rem`).
- **Internal Nested Elements:** Always subtract 4px from parent radius to maintain concentric visual harmony (e.g., an internal dossier preview panel inside a 16px card uses a 12px radius).
